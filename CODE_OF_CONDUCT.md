# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, technically honest, and generous with context. Critique ideas,
models, and evidence—not people. Scientific disagreements should identify the
assumptions, data, or numerical tests that would resolve them.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruptive conduct
are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this
standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report
conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through GitHub.
